🔢 PadBridge 1.0.2: the number of players always matches what you pick.

## ✨ What's new in 1.0.2
- 🔢 **Players match:** tapping **−** or **+** quickly could leave PadBridge's engine a step behind, so games saw 5 players while PadBridge showed 4. Every change now reaches the engine
- 🃏 **Roomier player cards:** with three or four players, the cards drop the "Games see…" line so the controller fits, even with bigger system text

---

## 🌉 What PadBridge does
PadBridge bridges the Thor's controls and your controllers onto steady player controllers, so you never remap your emulators again.
- 🔌 **Easy connecting:** pair a controller as usual and it lands on the next free player within a second. PadBridge remembers it next time
- 👥 **Up to 8 players:** cards side by side, in a square or in two rows, with **−** and **+** for how many players games always see
- 📱 **Who is Player 1:** Thor first, Controller first, or Both Player 1 (put the Thor down and pick up a controller mid-game)
- 🔁 **Swap:** flip Player 1 and 2 in one tap, or tap any two players to swap them
- 📌 **Fixed players:** pin a controller to a player, or leave it to Android
- 📳 **Rumble:** games' rumble reaches your controllers, and the Thor's motor for its own controls
- 🎛️ **Per-controller settings:** rumble strength, stick deadzone, invert right stick and Nintendo layout
- ⚡ **Speed readout:** how often each controller reports, and how little PadBridge adds
- 🧪 **Controller test:** every player drawn live on the top screen
- 📳 **Haptics:** pick a feel and strength for every action
- 🔄 **Updates:** check, see what's new, and install from Settings, or reinstall the current release

## 📲 Install
New to PadBridge? Download `PadBridge.apk` below, open it on the Thor to install, then open PadBridge and follow the setup steps (it needs [Shizuku](https://github.com/RikkaApps/Shizuku/releases/latest)).

Later updates: PadBridge tells you when a new version is out, or open Settings > **Check for updates**.
